Synopsis

About the world

On the lush alien world of Pandora live the Na'vi — beings who appear primitive but are highly evolved. Because the planet's air is toxic to humans, scientists deploy human/Na'vi hybrids called Avatars. Jake Sully, a paralyzed former Marine, is reborn in one such body and drawn into the fight for a world he has come to love.
