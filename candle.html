The choreography

A three-second story

On a 3 s loop the tall candle stretches, blinks sideways and blows a puff of smoke; the short candle wiggles, its wick twists and the flame dances bright, gutters and blows itself out. Freeze the flicker any time — the pause is one class and zero keyframes broken.
