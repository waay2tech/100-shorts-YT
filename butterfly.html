Engine notes

Born on the GPU

Every butterfly is a pair of phong-lit wings displaced by a shared noise field, integrated each frame on the graphics card. Position, velocity and attraction live in floating-point textures, so ten thousand wings can steer, zoom and slide without breaking a sweat.
